# How to find freelance bartender work in London

*This guide covers what to plan and ask now. The FireFlair service described as coming soon is not yet available, and this page will be updated when it is.*


London has a large events and hospitality scene, so there are many routes to freelance bar work. The routes are the same as elsewhere in the UK; the difference is volume and competition.

## Routes to work

| Route | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Agencies and bar companies** | Many events companies hire freelancers for private and corporate events |
| **Platforms** | Compare fees, commitment and payment handling: see [Indeed Flex alternatives](/stories/indeed-flex-alternatives-for-bartenders) |
| **Venues** | Regular shifts and cover work |
| **Your own network** | Other bartenders recommend people they trust |
| **FF Team** | A free Profile Card people can find and invite |

## Stand out

- A complete profile with real experience. See [building a bartender profile](/stories/how-to-build-a-bartender-profile).
- Reliability. See [your first event shift](/stories/first-event-bar-shift-checklist).
- Clear rates. See [setting your rates](/stories/how-to-set-freelance-bartender-rates).

## Travel

London shifts can finish late. Plan your journey home before you accept. See [what to check before accepting a shift](/stories/what-to-check-before-accepting-a-bar-shift).
