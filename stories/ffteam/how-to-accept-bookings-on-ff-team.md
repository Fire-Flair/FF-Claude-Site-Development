# How to receive and accept bookings on FF Team

Every request arrives as a card with the role, duties, place, date, hours, pay and terms. If you are invited by name, you can accept and confirm, decline or ask a question. If it comes from Autofill, replying "Available" tells the booker you are willing, and the booker then chooses.

## Read the card first

The card shows what you need to decide: role, duties, location, date, hours, pay, dress code, requirements and terms. Requests arrive on WhatsApp and on your Bookings page. The website is the official record.

## Two kinds of invitation

| | Invited by name (exclusive) | Autofill |
|---|---|---|
| Who gets it | You alone | A batch of matching professionals, normally ten |
| Your time to reply | Up to 24 hours, never beyond the shift start | Up to 24 hours |
| Accept & confirm | You are booked straight away, with no second approval | Not available |
| Available | Not needed | You tell the booker you can do it. You are **not** booked until they choose you |
| Discuss first | Ask a question. It does not confirm anything | Same. It does not extend any deadline |
| Decline | Frees the booker to invite someone else | Same |

Up to 24 hours is the time you have to reply. It is not a promise from FireFlair to answer you or confirm a booking within a day.

## Ask before you commit

Use Discuss first to check things such as transport, duties or whether pay can change. Only a person at FireFlair can change the pay or terms shown on the card.

## When you are confirmed

You and the booker receive the booking reference, shift details, profile links and the supported contact details. Your contact details are shared only once you are confirmed.

## If you cannot make it

Open the booking and choose Cancel shift. FireFlair is told straight away and works to find a replacement. Cancel as early as you can, because the booker is relying on you.

## After the event

Leave and receive reviews on the website. They become part of your profile and help you get booked again.
