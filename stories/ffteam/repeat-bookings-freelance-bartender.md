# How to get repeat bookings as a freelance bartender

Repeat work comes from being reliable and easy to rebook. Turn up prepared, thank the client, ask for a review and make your profile easy to share. Repeat business is likely, but it is never guaranteed.

## What brings people back

- **Preparation.** You knew the menu and the venue.
- **Reliability.** On time, in dress, and clear about the finish.
- **Communication.** You answered quickly before the event.

## After the event

1. Thank the client the same day.
2. Ask for a review on the website.
3. Ask permission before posting photos of the event.
4. Share your profile link, so they can invite you again.

## Keep in touch

A short message before their next occasion is fine. Do not send repeated unsolicited messages.
