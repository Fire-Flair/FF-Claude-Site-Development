# How to prepare for your first event bar shift

Read the brief twice, confirm the details the day before, arrive early and keep your contact person's number to hand. Events work differently from a regular bar job, so being prepared is what gets you remembered.

## The week before

- Read the booking card: place, time, duties, dress and requirements.
- Learn the menu. See [cocktail skills for event bartending](/stories/cocktail-skills-for-event-bartending).
- Check transport, parking and where to load.

## What to bring

- Your tools if the brief says so.
- Dress as the brief asks, with a spare shirt.
- A charger, ID and any licence information requested.

## On the day

1. **Arrive early.** Setup comes before guests.
2. **Meet your contact.** Know who to ask.
3. **Set up your station.** Ice, garnishes, glassware and a clean surface.
4. **Check the menu with the host.** Agree what is served and what is not.
5. **Finish properly.** Clear down, agree the finish time and confirm payment details.

## After

Leave and read reviews on the website. They help you get booked again. See [how to get repeat bookings](/stories/repeat-bookings-freelance-bartender).
