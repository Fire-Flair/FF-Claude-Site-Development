# What is FF Team and how does the FireFlair network work?

FireFlair is a network that connects bartenders and other hospitality professionals with the people who book them. FF Team is for professionals, FF Staff is for businesses that hire, and FF Party is for hosts arranging private celebrations. FireFlair starts with bartenders and is built by people with experience in the events industry around the world.

## The three sites

| Site | Who it is for | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **FF Team** | Bartenders and event professionals | Build a Profile Card and receive bookings |
| **FF Staff** | Bars, venues and event companies | Book staff for shifts |
| **FF Party** | Hosts of parties and celebrations | Plan an event and book your bar |

FireFlair is the name of the network, and the sites are run by FIREFLAIR LTD.

## How a booking works for you

1. You create a free Profile Card.
2. Hosts and businesses invite you to shifts, by name or through Autofill.
3. You see the details, pay and terms before you decide.
4. You accept, decline or ask a question, and you talk directly with the client once confirmed.

No percentage is taken from your agreed pay, and the booking fee is paid by the person booking.
