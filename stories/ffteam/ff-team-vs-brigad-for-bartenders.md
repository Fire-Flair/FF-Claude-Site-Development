# FF Team vs Brigad for freelance bartenders

Brigad is a hospitality freelance platform that handles missions, contracts, invoicing, payment options and insurance, and it charges talent a service fee. FF Team is built around a bartender's profile and direct relationships with the people who book them. This page is published by FireFlair, which runs FF Team, and describes Brigad from its public pages, checked on 8 November 2026.

## Side by side

| | Brigad | FF Team |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Hospitality missions across roles | Bartenders and other event professionals |
| **Admin** | Contracts, invoicing, payment options, insurance and support are described on its pages | You agree the booking and talk directly with the client |
| **Fee to you** | Its talent help page states a 12.9% service fee including VAT | No percentage deducted from agreed pay. The booking fee is paid by the person booking |
| **Business fee** | A separate business page describes a 10% mission fee | Not paid by you |
| **Your profile** | Check its current features | A shareable Profile Card with roles, areas, reviews and connections |
| **How you get work** | Missions you apply for or accept | Invitations to you by name, or Autofill batches |
| **Pay clarity** | Check each mission | Pay for each booking type is listed up front, for example £100 for a 3-hour bartending shift |

Brigad's services, such as invoicing and insurance, are a real benefit if you want the admin handled, so weigh them against the fee.

## Who may suit

**Brigad** may suit you if you want a managed set of services around each mission.

**FF Team** may suit you if you want to keep your agreed pay, build a profile people can find, and deal directly with clients.
