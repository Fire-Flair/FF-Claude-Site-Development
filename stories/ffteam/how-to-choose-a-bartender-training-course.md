# Bartender training courses in the UK and how to choose one

*This guide covers what to plan and ask now. The FireFlair service described as coming soon is not yet available, and this page will be updated when it is.*


Choose a course that gives you plenty of practice on a real bar, teaches the skills employers ask for and is honest about what it leads to. A certificate alone does not get you work.

## Questions to ask

- How many hours are hands-on?
- What will I be able to make and serve at the end?
- Is any qualification recognised, and by whom?
- Do I get help finding work, and how many past students are working?
- What is the full cost, including any kit?
