# How to find weekend bar work alongside another job

Be honest about when you are free, keep to what you agree, and leave time to rest. Weekend and evening event shifts suit people with another job, because many parties and receptions happen outside office hours.

## Plan it

- **Availability.** Choose the evenings and weekends you can really give.
- **Travel.** Count the journey home after a late finish.
- **Rest.** Do not stack shifts so tightly that your main job suffers.
- **Commitments.** Only accept what you can do.

## Where the work is

Private parties, weddings, corporate evenings and festivals. See [how to find freelance bartending work](/stories/how-to-find-freelance-bartending-work-uk).

## Check your contract

If you are employed, read your contract for restrictions on second jobs.

## Tax

Self-employed income from shifts counts as trading income. See [the trading allowance](/stories/freelance-bartending-trading-allowance) for the current rules and what is changing in April 2027.
