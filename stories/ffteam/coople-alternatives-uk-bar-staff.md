# Coople alternatives for UK bar staff

Coople closed its UK operations from 1 January 2026. If you used it for bar shifts, you can look at other platforms, specialist bar agencies and event companies, and you should contact Coople directly for old payslips or tax paperwork.

## Old paperwork

For pay or tax documents from your time on Coople, use Coople's own help pages for UK workers. Do not rely on any other site for those records.

## Where to look now

| Route | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Flexible-work platforms** | Indeed Flex, Brigad, limber, Stint | Compare fees, commitment and payment handling |
| **Bar specialists** | Off to Work, Wilde Toast, Viking Staffing & Events | Event bar work with a bar-focused team |
| **Event and festival staffing** | hap Solutions, Flair | Large events. Check location and vacancies |
| **FF Team** | Profile, invitations from hosts and businesses | Free profile; no percentage deducted from agreed pay |

## Before you switch

1. Check how and when you are paid.
2. Check what is deducted from your pay.
3. Ask whether you choose your shifts.
