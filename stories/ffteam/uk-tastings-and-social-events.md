# From Tastings to Music Bingo in the UK

A tasting gives everyone something to talk about as soon as the first drink lands. Music bingo gets a room listening for the same song. The format changes the feel of the evening before the first guest arrives.

## Two formats in the data

Eventbrite reported 80% more UK caffeine-tasting events and 49% higher attendance for that category on its platform. UK music bingo attendance rose 52%. The platform compared August 2024 to July 2025 with the previous 12 months. The figures describe Eventbrite's listings and attendance in these categories. Source: [Eventbrite Social Study 2026, pp. 12 and 25](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260114088488/en).

## Why formats matter to a bartender

A tasting and a music night can put hospitality professionals at the centre of very different rooms. If you're curious about a format, look at how local organisers describe the experience and the people behind it. Then think about which of your skills fit: guiding a tasting, running a busy bar between songs, or both.
