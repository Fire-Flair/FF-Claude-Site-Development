# Why Local Parties Matter to the Next Wave of Live Events

A neighbourhood get-together can have a character all its own. You're serving people who know the streets around them, and some of the faces across the room. Eventbrite's research offers two ways to look at that appetite for local events.

## People value the local connection

In Eventbrite's July 2025 survey, 88% of UK respondents aged 18–35 said it was important that an event helped them feel connected to their local community. This is a finding about what respondents value in an event. Source: [Eventbrite Social Study 2026, pp. 18 and 25](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260114088488/en).

## Outdoor party listings grew on Eventbrite

The platform reported 25% more UK listings in its backyard and local outdoor parties category between August 2024 and July 2025 than in the previous 12 months. The comparison used keywords in listings. For bartenders drawn to community events, that category offers a starting point for exploring the gatherings taking place close to home. Source: [Eventbrite Social Study 2026, pp. 18 and 25](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260114088488/en).

## Make it easy to be found locally

A listing count is not a promise of bookings. If local events appeal to you, say so on your profile: the areas you cover, the kinds of party you enjoy, and what you bring. See [how to build a bartender profile](/stories/how-to-build-a-bartender-profile).
