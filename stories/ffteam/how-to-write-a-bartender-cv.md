# How to write a bartender CV with examples

A bartender CV should be one page, lead with your recent experience, and show the skills a venue needs. A public profile complements it, because it can show reviews and photos that a CV cannot.

## Structure

1. **Name and contact.**
2. **Short profile.** Two lines: what you do and where.
3. **Experience.** Role, venue type, dates and one line on what you did.
4. **Skills.** Menus you can run, volume served, and languages.
5. **Training and licences.** For example a personal licence, if you hold one.
6. **References.** Offer them on request.

## Example experience entry (fictional)

> **Cocktail bartender, hotel bar, 2022 to 2025.** Served classic cocktails to 120 covers on busy nights, trained two new starters and led the weekly stock count.

Do not copy this wording. Use your own facts and keep every claim true.

## Profile or CV?

Use a CV when you apply to a bar or company. Use a profile when you want to be found and booked. See [how to build a bartender profile](/stories/how-to-build-a-bartender-profile).
