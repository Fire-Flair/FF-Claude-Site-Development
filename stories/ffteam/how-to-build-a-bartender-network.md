# How to build a bartender network and work with a team

Build your network by working well with other bartenders, keeping in touch and being a reliable name to recommend. A connection on a platform means you know each other. It does not prove you worked a particular event together.

## Why it helps

Event bars often need more than one person. Barbacks, bartenders and managers recommend people they trust.

## How to build it

- Work events with the same team where you can.
- Keep contact details of people you would work with again.
- Recommend others you trust, and ask for the same.
- Join trade events and shows, such as Bar Convent in London and Berlin.

## On FF Team

You can add people you have worked with to your profile, and the people who book you can see who you work alongside. Reviews and references should come from real work.

## Roles in a team

Know the difference between a bartender, a barback and a manager. See [barback, bartender or bar manager](https://ffstaff.uk/stories/barback-bartender-or-bar-manager).
