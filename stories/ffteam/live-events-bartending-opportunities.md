# What Younger Guests Want From a Night Out

A pop-up, an after-hours museum night, and a hidden bar can all give someone a reason to head out. Eventbrite's 2026 Social Study puts numbers to that curiosity.

## The appetite for a night out

In July 2025, Eventbrite's research partner dcdx surveyed 4,051 people aged 18–35 across the US and UK. Of those surveyed, 79% expected to attend more events in 2026. The figure was 82% for UK respondents. These figures capture people's plans at the time of the survey. Source: [Eventbrite Social Study 2026, pp. 4 and 25](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260114088488/en).

## What people wanted to find

Asked about hard-to-find events they would be interested in attending, 40% picked pop-up experiences or installations and 36% picked speakeasies or underground bars. These are preferences from the combined survey. For a bartender, they offer a glimpse of the settings in which guests want to spend an evening. Source: [Eventbrite Social Study 2026, p. 6](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260114088488/en).

## What it could mean for your profile

Interest in a format is not a vacancy, and nothing here promises work. It does show where guests are looking. A clear [FF Team profile](/stories/how-to-build-a-bartender-profile) that shows the kinds of events you enjoy working gives hosts and event businesses something to find.
