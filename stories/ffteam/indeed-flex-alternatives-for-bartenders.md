# Indeed Flex alternatives for bartenders in the UK

This page is published by FireFlair, which runs FF Team. It describes other services from their own public pages as checked on 7 November 2026. Indeed Flex is a large, established route with a dedicated bartender section, so the aim here is to show what else exists and who each option may suit.

## How this list was chosen

Services that advertise bar or hospitality work in the UK, that you can join as an individual, and whose pages describe how they work. Overseas-only services and services that have closed in the UK are left out.

## The options

| Option | What its pages describe | May suit you if |
|---|---|---|
| **Indeed Flex** | Temporary work across sectors, a bartender route, flexible schedules, verification and benefits | You want broad choice of shifts and an established process. Check eligibility for benefits and how pay is handled |
| **Brigad** | Hospitality freelance missions, contracts, invoicing, payment options and insurance. A talent service fee is stated on its help page | You want the admin handled and have checked the fee and what it covers |
| **limber** | Hospitality staffing, favourite worker teams, payment and payroll handling | You want to be added to a business's regular team |
| **Stint** | Recurring short shifts in hospitality. Current help material describes a commitment of at least three months | You want regular, repeating shifts rather than one-offs |
| **Specialist bar agencies** | Dedicated bar divisions covering barbacks, bartenders, flair and managers, for example Off to Work and Wilde Toast | You want event bar work with a bar-focused team |
| **Event and festival staffing** | For example hap Solutions and Flair, covering festivals, stadiums and large events | You want large-event work. Check location and current vacancies |
| **FF Team** | A bartender-first profile, shareable by link, with invitations from event hosts and businesses | You want to show your craft, build repeat relationships and see the brief before you commit. It is new and does not promise shifts |

Coople closed its UK operations from 1 January 2026.

## Questions to ask any of them

1. Who pays me, when, and what is taken from my pay?
2. How much commitment is required?
3. Do I choose my shifts?
4. Can I build a public profile or reputation?
5. Am I treated as self-employed or as a worker, and does that match the reality of the work? See [freelance or agency work](/stories/freelance-bartending-or-agency-bar-work).
