# Cocktail skills to practise for event bartending

Event bartending rewards consistency and speed more than a huge repertoire. Practise a small set of classics until you can make them quickly and the same every time, learn to set up a station, and be ready with good alcohol-free choices.

## The classics to know

Customers book with a cocktail menu in hand. The UK's best-loved classics include the Margarita, Pornstar Martini, Mojito and Espresso Martini. Knowing these well makes you the professional they remember.

## Practise for consistency

- Learn recipes by measure, not by guess.
- Time yourself on a set of five drinks.
- Taste each drink, to check balance.

## Mise en place

Prepare garnishes, syrups and juices before service, and set out ice, glassware and tools so you can work without leaving your station.

## Service workflow

- Take the order, make the drink, serve and clear in a steady rhythm.
- Keep the bar clean as you go.
- Communicate with a barback if you have one.

## Alcohol-free

Practise at least two good mocktails. Guests who do not drink should get the same care.
