# How to brief a bespoke guest itinerary

A good brief makes a day or an evening easy to arrange. With FF Partners you can send one brief for a guest, with several elements in it, without listing a property.

## Start with the occasion and the place

Say what is being celebrated, where it takes place and when. If the date is not fixed yet, say the dates are flexible. A place can be a town, a region or a country.

## Describe the whole day or evening

List the elements you would like: a private dinner, a sommelier-led tasting, a cocktail masterclass, a vineyard visit, a boat trip or a private tour. Put them in the order you picture them, and add timings where you have them.

## Share preferences and language

Add what the guest enjoys and any dietary needs. If the guest prefers a particular language, say so, and FireFlair will look for people who can help in it.

## Say how to reply

Add the best way to reach you. Add the guest's contact only if you have it and the arrangement needs it.

## What happens next

Your brief is an enquiry. FireFlair reviews it and replies with what can be arranged. Where an FF Party package fits, the booking continues through the usual FF Party flow. Accommodation, transport tickets and travel stay with the relevant operator.
