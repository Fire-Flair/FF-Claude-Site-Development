# Creating a cocktail experience at home

Your kitchen can be the best bar in town. With a little imagination, an evening at home can feel as special as a night out, and a lot more personal.

## Make it hands-on

People love making things together. When everyone has a shaker in their hands, the evening stops being about waiting for drinks and becomes the activity itself. Laughter comes naturally when the first shake goes slightly wrong.

## Let everyone create

The Open Bar Masterclass is built on that idea. A host mixologist guides your group while everyone shakes up the cocktails they want and creates their own recipes. Nobody sits on the sidelines, and everybody leaves with a favourite.

## Set the scene

Clear a table, light some candles, and keep music playing at a level where people can talk. Think about glassware and ice in advance, so the first shake is a celebration rather than a search through cupboards.

## Share the results

Taste, compare and vote. A friendly competition for the best drink turns a good evening into a great one.
