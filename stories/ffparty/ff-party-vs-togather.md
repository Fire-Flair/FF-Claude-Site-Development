# FF Party vs Togather for party bar services

Togather matches your event brief with suppliers, with quotes and messaging, and describes payment support. FF Party also starts from your brief, and focuses on bar services with the FireFlair team behind it. This page is published by FireFlair, which runs FF Party, and describes Togather from its public pages, checked on 8 November 2026.

## Side by side

| | Togather | FF Party |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Many event services | Bar services and event staff |
| **Brief** | You submit your event brief | You describe your event and choose your service |
| **Matching** | Technology and event expertise match suppliers | Professionals matched on role, work area and distance, with the FireFlair team helping on larger events |
| **Contact** | Quotes and messaging | Your booking page, WhatsApp and email notifications |
| **Payment** | Describes payment support | £50 deposit, fully refunded if we cannot find the staff; professionals' pay shown separately |

## Who may suit

**Togather** may suit you if you are planning a wider event and want help across several services.

**FF Party** may suit you if your focus is the bar and you want people with global events industry experience to arrange it.
