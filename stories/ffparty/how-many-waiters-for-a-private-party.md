# How many waiters do I need for a private party?

*This guide covers what to plan and ask now. The FireFlair service described as coming soon is not yet available, and this page will be updated when it is.*


The number of waiters depends on your style of service. A seated meal needs more staff per guest than canapés and drinks. Treat any figure as a starting point and ask your supplier.

## Think about

- **Service style:** seated, buffet, canapés or drinks only
- **Guest numbers**
- **Length and number of courses**
- **Venue layout:** distance from the kitchen, stairs, number of rooms

## Typical approach

Seated, multi-course service needs more staff for each guest than a buffet. Ask a catering or staffing supplier how many they recommend for your plan, since they know their own service.
