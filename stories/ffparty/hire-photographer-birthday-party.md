# How to hire a photographer for a birthday party

*This guide covers what to plan and ask now. The FireFlair service described as coming soon is not yet available, and this page will be updated when it is.*


Agree what you want covered, how long, and when you will get the pictures. Ask to see a portfolio from a similar event.

## Agree

- **Coverage:** arrivals, speeches, the cake, group photos, candid shots
- **Hours:** the photographer's start and end time
- **Delivery:** how many edited photos and when
- **Usage:** whether the photographer may share images, and whether you may print them
- **Guests:** let guests know a photographer will be there

## Practical

Brief them on the schedule and tell your bartender and other suppliers they will be around. Allow time for a group shot before guests disperse.
