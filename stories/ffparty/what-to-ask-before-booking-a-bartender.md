# What to Ask Before Booking a Bartender for a Private Party

Before booking a bartender, ask about experience with your kind of party, what the service includes, and how your booking is confirmed. A bartender at a birthday might serve a signature cocktail as guests arrive and keep the bar running through the evening. At a masterclass, the same professional might spend their time guiding guests through the drinks. Tell the person you're considering what you want your guests to experience. Their answers will help you choose someone who suits the occasion.

## Have you worked on a party like mine?

Share the occasion, guest numbers, location, and the hours you'd like drinks served. Look at examples of their work and any relevant reviews. Ask how they'd handle arrivals if most guests come at once, and what sort of menu they'd suggest for your space and timings.

## What will each of us provide?

Ask who brings ingredients, ice, glassware, tools, and a bar setup. Check how much setup space they need and whether setup and clear-down are included in the agreed hours or price. If you already have a favourite drink in mind, check whether they can serve it and offer an alcohol-free option alongside it. For a full checklist, see [what to provide when you hire a bartender](/stories/what-to-provide-when-hiring-a-bartender).

## What will the booking cover?

Before you commit, review the service, hours, inclusions, and total cost. Check what you need to pay, when the booking becomes confirmed, and how to contact the professional if the plan changes. A short written brief with the agreed details gives you both something to refer back to before the event.
