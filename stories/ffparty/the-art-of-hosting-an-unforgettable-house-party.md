# The art of hosting an unforgettable house party

Picture the moment your guests walk in: music low, glasses catching the light, and a drink in every hand before the coats are off. That first five minutes decides how the whole night feels.

## Start with the welcome

Greet people with something in their hand. A first drink says you are glad they came, and it gives everyone something to hold while conversations begin. Decide your welcome drink in advance, and keep it simple enough that it can be made quickly and well.

## Give the bar a home

A bar needs a spot of its own, with a surface, space to stand and a little light. Put it away from the front door so guests flow through your home instead of gathering in the hallway. A good bar becomes the heart of the party, so give it room.

## Let someone else look after the drinks

The best hosts are in the room, not behind the bar. A professional bartender lets you toast, introduce friends and enjoy your own evening. That is the idea behind the Bartending Package: a bartender, your bar set up and the drinks served, so you can be a guest at your own party.

## Keep a rhythm

Open with something light, build towards the main event, and finish with a drink that says goodnight. Parties that have a gentle shape feel effortless, even when someone has planned every detail.
