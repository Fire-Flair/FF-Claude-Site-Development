# A Signature Cocktail for the Whole Party

Pick one drink that matches your occasion and that a bartender can make quickly in volume. That single choice becomes the party's signature, and it makes the rest of the planning easier.

## Start with the occasion

Think about what the party is celebrating, the season and the mood. A summer garden party calls for something fresh and long. An autumn dinner suits something warm and rounded. A drink that fits the moment feels intentional to guests.

## Add a flavour your group already loves

If your guests enjoy citrus, berries, coffee or ginger, begin there. A familiar flavour in a new combination feels special without being unfamiliar.

## Make it easy to serve

A signature drink works best when it can be prepared the same way many times. Tell the bartender how many guests you expect and when drinks begin, and they can advise on whether a recipe suits the pace of your evening. For quantities, see [how many cocktails to plan for your guests](/stories/how-many-cocktails-for-a-party).

## Offer an alcohol-free match

Many hosts like a matching alcohol-free version of the signature drink, so everyone shares in the theme. Another option is a separate alcohol-free drink with its own flavour. Choose the one that suits your guests.

## A simple brief

This example is illustrative:

| Brief item | Example |
|---|---|
| Occasion | A summer birthday in the garden |
| Flavour | Citrus and mint |
| Timing | Served as guests arrive, then through the first hour |
| Servings | Guest numbers shared with the bartender in advance |
| Alcohol-free | A matching version with the same garnish |

## A note on recipes

If you book a bar company, their signature recipes may belong to them. The company decides which recipes it makes available, so ask what is on offer. To build a menu around several drinks, read [how to plan a cocktail menu for a party](/stories/how-to-plan-a-cocktail-menu-for-a-party).
