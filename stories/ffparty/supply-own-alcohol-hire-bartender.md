# Can I supply my own alcohol when hiring a bartender?

Often yes, but agree it with both your supplier and your venue first. Some suppliers offer a bartender-only service for this, while others price a package that includes drinks.

## Check three things

1. **Your supplier.** Do they work with client-supplied drinks, and do they want a list?
2. **Your venue.** Some venues do not allow it, or charge corkage.
3. **Licensing.** Check what applies to your event. Temporary event notice rules apply in England and Wales, and other parts of the UK differ.

## Planning your stock

- Ask your bartender what to buy for your menu. See [how many cocktails to plan](/stories/how-many-cocktails-for-a-party).
- Plan mixers, ice, garnishes and glassware too.
- Store drinks cold and out of the way, and agree where the bartender will find them.

## Leftovers

Decide in advance who keeps unopened bottles.

## The trade-off

Buying your own can save effort in some cases and cost in others. A package that includes drinks is simpler, with one price and one supplier. See [bartender hire vs mobile bar hire](/stories/bartender-hire-vs-mobile-bar-hire).
