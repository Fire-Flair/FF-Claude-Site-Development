# Cash bar or open bar for a wedding?

In an open bar the hosts pay, and in a cash bar the guests pay for their own drinks. Many weddings choose something in between, such as drinks included for the reception and a cash bar in the evening. Check licensing and the venue's rules before you decide.

## Compare

| | Open bar | Limited open bar | Cash bar |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Who pays** | You | You, for a set period or set drinks | Guests |
| **Guest experience** | Everyone served freely | Welcome drinks and toasts covered | Guests pay as they go |
| **Planning** | A budget or minimum spend | A defined number of drinks or hours | Often a minimum spend from the supplier |

## Things to check

- **Licensing.** A temporary event notice may be needed in England and Wales when the venue does not hold a suitable licence. Other parts of the UK differ, so check with your venue or council.
- **Minimum spend.** Some bar companies set one for a cash bar.
- **Payment method.** Card payments for guests.
- **Alcohol-free options** for those not drinking.
