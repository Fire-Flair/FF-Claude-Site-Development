# Setting the atmosphere for your celebration

Atmosphere is the difference between a room full of people and a party. It is light, sound, scent and the drink in your hand, working together without anyone noticing.

## Start with light

Lower the main lights, add warm lamps or candles, and avoid anything harsh. Soft light flatters everyone and invites people to relax.

## Choose the sound

Music should support conversation, not compete with it. Start gentle, build as the night warms up, and let the volume follow the mood of the room.

## Make the drinks part of the scene

A well-run bar adds energy: the clink of glasses, the shake of a shaker, a bartender who knows when to add a little extra. Drinks served with care set the tone for everything else.

## Think about the finish

Plan how the evening ends: a last round, a slower playlist, a thank-you. People remember endings.
