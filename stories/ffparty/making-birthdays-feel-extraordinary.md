# Making birthdays feel extraordinary

Ask people about their best birthday and they rarely mention the cake. They remember how it felt: the friends in one room, the toast, the one small detail that was clearly made just for them.

## Choose one thing that is personal

A signature cocktail named after the guest of honour, a drink inspired by a favourite place, a garnish that tells a story. One personal detail does more than a dozen generic ones.

## Build the menu around the person

Think about what they love: smoky or fresh, sweet or dry, classic or adventurous. Two or three cocktails with a clear character are better than a long list. Your guests will talk about them the next day.

## Bring in a mixologist

This is where the Mixology Package shines. A mixologist creates a bespoke cocktail menu around your celebration, with garnishes and a shopping list so you know exactly what to have ready. You choose the mood; they make it taste like the person you are celebrating.

## Finish with a toast

End the evening with a toast everyone can join in. Hand out the last drinks, say a few words, and let the room do the rest.
