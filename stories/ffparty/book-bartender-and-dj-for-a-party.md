# How to book a bartender and DJ for the same party

*This guide covers what to plan and ask now. The FireFlair service described as coming soon is not yet available, and this page will be updated when it is.*


Treat them as one evening plan. Agree a timeline that suits both, check space and power, and tell each supplier about the other.

## Coordinate

- **Timeline:** when each arrives, sets up and finishes
- **Space:** bar and DJ positions that keep queues away from the dance floor
- **Power:** enough sockets, and who brings extension leads
- **Volume:** a level that lets guests talk at the bar
- **Contact:** one person on the day who both can reach

## Booking

Book the bar first if your guest numbers drive the rest. See [how to book on FF Party](/stories/how-to-book-a-bartender-on-ff-party).
