# Small parties with big personalities

A small party can feel bigger than a big one. Twelve friends, one room and the right spark, and the night has more character than any crowd of hundreds.

## Aim for one moment people will retell

Every memorable small party has a moment: the toast, the surprise, the song everyone sang. Plan the moment, and the rest of the evening takes care of itself.

## Add a little show

Flair bartending is theatre you can taste. Bottles and shakers move through the air, glasses are filled with a flourish, and guests who came for a drink stay for the performance. It gives your party a centrepiece without needing a stage.

## Keep it close

In a small space, guests are near the action. That is a strength. They see the technique, they chat with the bartender, and the whole room is part of it.
