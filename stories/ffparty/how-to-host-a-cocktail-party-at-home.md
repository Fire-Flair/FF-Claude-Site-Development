# How to Host a Cocktail Party at Home

To host a cocktail party at home, choose a short drinks menu, set up one place to serve it, and plan when you want the first round ready. Start with the moment you want everyone to share. For a birthday, that might be the first toast. For dinner with friends, it could be cocktails before you sit down. If you want the drinks to be the activity, make a cocktail masterclass part of the evening and give it a start time.

## Choose a short drinks menu

Pick two or three drinks that suit the occasion. You could offer something bright and citrusy, a favourite of the guest of honour, and an alcohol-free option with its own garnish. A short menu gives each drink a place in the evening and makes the shopping list easier to work out. Agree the menu and who's supplying the ingredients before you shop.

## Give the bar a place of its own

Choose a counter or table guests can approach while leaving space for others to move past. Think about where glassware, ice, and spare stock will go, and where guests can help themselves to water. If you're bringing in a bartender, share a photo of the space and agree when they can arrive to set up. Tell them how many guests you expect and when most people are likely to arrive.

## Make time for the party itself

A bartender can prepare and serve the drinks while you greet your guests. A masterclass gives the group a shared activity, with someone to guide each round. Choose the format that fits the evening, then put the drinks, food, and any toast on a simple timeline. When the first drinks are ready, make sure you're there to raise a glass too.
