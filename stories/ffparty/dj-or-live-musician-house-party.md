# DJ or live musician for a house party?

*This guide covers what to plan and ask now. The FireFlair service described as coming soon is not yet available, and this page will be updated when it is.*


A DJ suits dancing and a long night; live musicians suit atmosphere and a more listening-led evening. Space, noise limits and equipment usually decide it.

## Compare

| | DJ | Live musicians |
|---|---|---|
| **Space** | One small setup | A band needs room |
| **Noise** | Adjustable | Often louder to start |
| **Range** | Any genre, all night | Fixed repertoire |
| **Mood** | Dancing | Atmosphere |
| **Power and kit** | Speakers and power | Amps, instruments, power |

## Check

- Your venue's noise rules and finish time
- Power points and where the act will stand
- How long you need music, and for which part of the evening

## Drinks

Whichever you choose, plan your bar around it. See [how to hire a bartender for a house party](/stories/how-to-hire-a-bartender-for-a-house-party).
