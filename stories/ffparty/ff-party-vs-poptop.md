# FF Party vs Poptop for bartender hire

Poptop offers instant quotes for relevant services, takes a deposit, and the supplier then confirms. FF Party works from your brief, with the FireFlair team helping to confirm the people you want. This page is published by FireFlair, which runs FF Party, and describes Poptop from its public pages, checked on 8 November 2026.

## Side by side

| | Poptop | FF Party |
|---|---|---|
| **Starting point** | Enter your event details and see quotes | Describe your event and choose your service |
| **Stages** | Instant quote, deposit, supplier confirmation | Request, deposit, invitation, confirmation |
| **Supplier fee** | Its help pages describe a 12% supplier fee including VAT | Not applicable to you as a host |
| **Your deposit** | Taken as part of booking on the platform | £50, fully refunded if we cannot find the staff |
| **Menu planning** | Discuss with the supplier | Choose cocktails and servings in the planner |
| **Larger events** | Choose from suppliers listed | The FireFlair team can contact bar companies for you |

An instant quote and a confirmed booking are different stages, so check which one you are looking at on any platform.

## Who may suit

**Poptop** may suit you if you want visible prices quickly.

**FF Party** may suit you if you want a complete booking, with your drinks menu planned and an experienced team behind it.
