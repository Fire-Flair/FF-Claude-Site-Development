# How to offer a private cocktail experience at a holiday let

A private cocktail experience is an easy extra to offer guests who are celebrating at your property. A mixologist or a cocktail masterclass comes to the property, and your guests enjoy the evening without leaving. FF Partners lets you share one guest page that sets it all out.

## Decide what you will offer

Choose the FF Party experiences you are happy to put your name to. A private mixologist and a cocktail masterclass suit most properties. Each one carries its own duration and inclusions, so guests know what to expect before they book.

## Tell guests what to expect before they arrive

Put the guest link in your pre-arrival guide, so the experience can be planned before the stay begins. Share the things guests ask most: how many people the space suits, whether visitors can join, when the evening needs to finish, and whether there is room to set up a bar.

## Ask about guest preferences

When a guest books, FireFlair collects the occasion, the number of guests and their preferences on the Event Card. Encourage guests to say what they enjoy, any dietary needs and whether the group would like to learn or simply be served.

## Plan the handover

Agree who supplies the drinks, ice and glassware, and write it in your property answers so the professional and the guest see the same thing. Keep the exact address and access instructions in your private details. They are shared only with the people working at the property.

## Work with local suppliers

For extras such as a private chef, a sommelier or a local outing, send FireFlair a brief and the request is arranged with the right specialist. Accommodation, availability and payment stay with you.
