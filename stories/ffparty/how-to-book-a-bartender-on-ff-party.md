# How to book a bartender on FF Party

You describe your event, pay FireFlair's booking charge, and FF Party invites bartenders who fit. A booking is confirmed only when a bartender accepts, or when you choose one who has said they are available. Until then you have a request, not a booking.

## What you need before you start

Your phone number and email, the date, place, start and finish times, guest numbers, and the kind of service you want. You do not need a company account.

## The steps

1. **Describe the event.** Occasion, guests, hours and location. The event is saved as an Event Card.
2. **Say what you need.** Each role you need becomes a Shift Card on the event, such as one bartender or two. Each position is tracked separately.
3. **Pay FireFlair's booking charge.** It is taken before invitations go out and is separate from the bartender's pay, which is shown on its own line.
4. **Invite.** You can invite a bartender by name, which is an exclusive invitation, or let Autofill invite a batch of matching professionals.
5. **Choose and confirm.** See "What happens next" below.

## What happens next

**Exclusive invitation.** The bartender you chose has up to 24 hours to reply, and never beyond the start of the shift. They can accept and confirm, decline, or ask a question. If they accept, the booking is confirmed without a second approval from you. The invitation is exclusive for that shift. It does not reserve their whole diary.

**Autofill.** Invitations go to a batch of matching professionals, normally ten. "Available" is a reply, not a confirmation: you then pick the person you want. Choosing "Discuss first" does not confirm anything and does not extend any deadline. Each invitation has its own selection deadline, normally the earlier of seven days after it was created and the start of the shift. A reply does not restart those seven days.

## Does 24 hours mean I get an answer in 24 hours?

No. It is the time a bartender has to respond. It is not a promise of a reply or of a confirmed booking within a day. If you are booking close to the date, say so in the request and keep a second option in mind.

## After you are confirmed

You both receive the booking details and the supported contact information. Your booking page on the website is the official record; WhatsApp and email carry notifications and quick replies. You can leave a review on the website after the event.

## If something changes

If a confirmed bartender cancels, the position is reopened and a replacement is sought through the same process, with no further replacement-matching charge. A replacement depends on availability. For refunds, see [how FF Party booking deposits and refunds work](/stories/ff-party-booking-deposits-and-refunds).
