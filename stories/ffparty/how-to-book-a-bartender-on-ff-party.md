# How to book a bartender on FF Party

Tell us about your event, choose the service you want, and pay a £50 deposit. FF Party then invites bartenders who fit your brief, and you are booked when one confirms. The FireFlair team has worked in the events industry around the world, and we make sure the bar suits the occasion.

## What you need before you start

Your phone number and email, the date, place, start and finish times, guest numbers, and the kind of night you want. You do not need an account for a company.

## The steps

1. **Describe the event.** Occasion, guests, hours and location. It is saved as your Event Card.
2. **Choose your service.** A bartender, a cocktail bar service, a masterclass, a flair performance, or a team for a bigger night.
3. **Pay the £50 deposit.** It secures your request and is fully refunded if we cannot find the staff for your event.
4. **Choose your people.** Invite a bartender you like by name, or let Autofill invite professionals who match.
5. **Confirm.** See below.

## What happens next

**Invited by name.** The bartender has up to 24 hours to reply, and never beyond the start of the event. If they accept, you are booked, with no second approval from you. The invitation is exclusive for your event; it does not reserve their whole diary.

**Autofill.** Invitations go to a batch of matching professionals, normally ten. "Available" is a reply, not a booking: you then pick the person you want. Choosing "Discuss first" does not confirm anything and does not extend any deadline. Each invitation has its own deadline, normally the earlier of seven days after it was created and the start of the event.

## Does 24 hours mean I get an answer in 24 hours?

No. It is the time a professional has to reply. It is not a promise of a reply or of a confirmed booking within a day. If your date is close, say so in your request.

## Want a bar company for a bigger night?

For larger events, the FireFlair team can contact bar companies for you. See [how to compare mobile bar companies](/stories/how-to-compare-mobile-bar-companies).

## After you are confirmed

You both receive the booking details and the supported contact information. Your booking page on the website is the official record, and WhatsApp and email carry notifications. You can leave a review on the website after the event.

## If your plans change

You can change your event date up to 30 days before. If a confirmed bartender cancels, we reopen the position and look for a replacement with no extra matching fee. See [how the deposit and refunds work](/stories/ff-party-booking-deposits-and-refunds).
