# Dry bar hire, staffed bar hire and full bar service explained

"Dry hire" is used in different ways by different suppliers, so always read what is included. Commonly it means the bar and equipment without the drinks, but some suppliers use it to mean the bar with bartenders and no alcohol, and others mean only the equipment.

## Three common levels

| Level | Usually includes | You usually provide |
|---|---|---|
| **Dry hire** | A bar unit, sometimes equipment | Drinks, staff, often ice and glassware |
| **Staffed bar** | A bar and bartenders | Alcohol and sometimes ingredients |
| **Full bar service** | Bar, staff, stock, glassware and ice | Space and access |

## Scope checklist

Ask each supplier to confirm, in writing:

- Does the price include bartenders, and how many?
- Who provides alcohol, mixers and garnishes?
- Who provides ice and glassware, and who collects and washes it?
- Is travel, setup and clearing included?
- Is there a minimum spend?

## For bigger events

For 100 guests or more we usually recommend a bar company, which can provide the bar, stock, glassware and ice at your request. The FireFlair team can contact bar companies for you. See [how to compare mobile bar companies](/stories/how-to-compare-mobile-bar-companies).
