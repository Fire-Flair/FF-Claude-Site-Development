# How to book a cocktail masterclass at home

*This guide covers what to plan and ask now. The FireFlair service described as coming soon is not yet available, and this page will be updated when it is.*


A masterclass is hands-on: your group learns to make drinks with an expert. Agree the group size, duration, what is supplied and what you need to provide.

## Agree

- **Group size and the number of drinks each person makes**
- **Duration**
- **Equipment:** who brings shakers, tools and glassware
- **Ingredients and alcohol:** what is included
- **Space:** tables and water nearby
- **Alcohol-free options** for those who prefer them
