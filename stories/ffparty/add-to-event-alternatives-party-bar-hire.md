# Add to Event alternatives for party bar hire

This page is published by FireFlair, which runs FF Party. Details about other services come from their own public pages, checked on 7 November 2026. Each route below can suit a different host, and what a page does not mention is not evidence that a service lacks it.

## The main routes

| Route | How it works, per its public pages | May suit you if |
|---|---|---|
| **Add to Event** | You request quotes and compare suppliers, reviews and photos. Suppliers buy credits to contact selected leads and pay no booking commission. | You want several quotes and are happy to compare them yourself. |
| **Poptop** | Offers instant quotes for relevant services, deposit payment and supplier confirmation. Its help pages describe a supplier fee. | You want visible prices and an online deposit. An instant quote and a confirmed booking are different stages. |
| **Togather** | Matches an event brief with suppliers, with quotes, messaging and payment support. | You want a brief-led, curated route. |
| **Bark** | A broad services marketplace. Professionals pay credits to contact leads, and customers compare profiles and reviews. | You want a broad marketplace for many kinds of service. |
| **Direct bar companies** | Examples include Mix & Twist, Cocktailhire, Hire A Barman and Cocktail Barmen, who publish their own packages. | You know what you want and prefer a single specialist operator. |
| **FF Party** | You describe the event, choose the people you want, and pay a £50 deposit that is fully refunded if we cannot find the staff. For a mobile bar or full package, the FireFlair team, with global events industry experience, contacts bartenders and bar companies for your brief and brings you the options. | You want a complete booking that is confirmed with you, and want experienced people to do the first round of enquiries. Options depend on who is free for your date and area. |

## How to choose

Use the same criteria for each: service type, team, menu, supplies, total cost and how a booking is confirmed. The [comparison worksheet](/stories/how-to-compare-mobile-bar-companies) lays them out.
