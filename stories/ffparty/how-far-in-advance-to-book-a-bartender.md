# How far in advance should I book a bartender?

Book as soon as you know your date, particularly for popular times such as summer weekends, December and weddings. A request sent earlier gives you more choice, and on FF Party you can change the date up to 30 days before the event if your plans move.

## What affects lead time

- **The season.** Festive dates and summer weekends fill first.
- **The service.** A specific person or a full bar company is booked further ahead than a simple bartender.
- **Event size.** Larger events need more people to be free at once.

## Have this ready

Date, place, start and finish, guest numbers and the kind of service you want. The more complete your request, the faster we can invite people.

## If your date is close

Say so in your request and keep a backup in mind. When we invite someone, they have up to 24 hours to reply, which is the time they have to answer, not a promise of a reply or a confirmed booking within a day.

## Can I move my date?

Yes. You can book now and change the date up to 30 days before. See [how the deposit works](/stories/ff-party-booking-deposits-and-refunds).
