# Cocktail bartender or cocktail masterclass for a hen party?

Choose a cocktail bartender if you want your group to relax and be served. Choose a masterclass if you want an activity where everyone makes their own drinks. Many hen parties do the class first and then a bartender for the evening.

## Side by side

| | Cocktail bartender | Cocktail masterclass |
|---|---|---|
| **What happens** | Your bartender makes drinks for guests | A host teaches the group to make drinks |
| **Feel** | Drinks party | Interactive activity |
| **Length** | Whole evening or a set period | Usually 1½ to 2 hours |
| **Group size** | Any, with more staff for larger groups | Best for groups who can share a table |
| **Good for** | Parties at home or at a venue | Hen and stag parties, team socials |

## What is included

On FF Party a Masterclass Package includes a host mixologist, all equipment and three to four cocktails each. A Cocktail Bar Service writes and follows a cocktail menu for your event.

## Questions to ask

- How many people can take part at once?
- Who provides ingredients and glassware?
- Is there an alcohol-free option for guests who want one?
- Do we need space for everyone to stand at a table?
