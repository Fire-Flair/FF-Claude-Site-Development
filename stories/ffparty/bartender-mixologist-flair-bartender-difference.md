# Bartender, mixologist or flair bartender: what is the difference?

A bartender serves drinks, a mixologist focuses on crafting cocktails, and a flair bartender adds a performance. None of these is a regulated title, so judge by experience and what the person can show you.

## What each usually means

| Term | Usual meaning | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Bartender** | Serves a range of drinks efficiently | House parties, busy bars, general service |
| **Mixologist** | Specialises in crafted cocktails and menu building | Weddings, launches and cocktail-led events |
| **Flair bartender** | Adds juggling and showmanship to service | Events that want a show |

## What to ask

- What menus have you run?
- How many guests have you served alone?
- Can you share photos or reviews?
