# How to hire a cocktail bartender in London

*This guide covers what to plan and ask now. The FireFlair service described as coming soon is not yet available, and this page will be updated when it is.*


In London the hard part is often logistics, not finding a bartender. Access, parking, lifts and venue rules can decide whether a cocktail service runs smoothly.

## Logistics to settle early

- **Access.** Can equipment, ice and stock be brought in easily? A fourth-floor flat with no lift changes what a bartender can bring.
- **Parking and loading.** Ask your supplier whether they need a bay or a permit near the venue.
- **Space.** A cocktail bar needs a clear surface, water nearby and room to work.
- **Timing.** Venue curfews and neighbours affect setup and finish times.
