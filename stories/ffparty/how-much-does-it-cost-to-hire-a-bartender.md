# How much does it cost to hire a bartender?

The price depends on what is included. A bartender alone, a bartender with ingredients, a staffed mobile bar and a fully stocked package are different services, so compare what each quote covers before comparing totals. Published prices are starting points set by each supplier, and no single figure can stand for the UK.

## What changes the total

- **Hours of service**, including setup and clearing, not only the time guests are drinking.
- **Number of staff.** A larger or busier party needs more than one person. See [how many bartenders you need](/stories/how-many-bartenders-do-i-need).
- **Skills.** Classic cocktails, flair and a plain bar service are different jobs.
- **Equipment**, such as a bar, tools, glassware and ice.
- **Ingredients and alcohol**, and who supplies them.
- **Travel, delivery and parking.**
- **Minimum spend or minimum hours.**
- **VAT**, where the supplier charges it.

## Ask every supplier the same questions

1. Is this the person only, the person with equipment, or a full package?
2. Are ingredients, ice and glassware included?
3. What is the minimum booking, and does travel cost extra?
4. Is VAT included in the price I am shown?
5. How is my booking confirmed, and what happens if the bartender cannot attend?

The [supplies checklist](/stories/what-to-provide-when-hiring-a-bartender) turns this into a list for your own venue.

## Supplier examples (checked 7 November 2026)

These are what each supplier or platform advertises. They are not a UK average, and they cover different things, so read the last column.

| Source | Advertised price | What it describes |
|---|---|---|
| Poptop, London bartender listings | "From £120 per event"; individual listings from £210 and £270 per event | Platform listings for bartenders in London; the final price depends on guests, hours and menu |
| Poptop, UK cocktail bars | £33 per guest for all-inclusive drinks packages; around £325 per event for dry-hire cash bars (platform-reported averages) | Cocktail bar suppliers on the platform |
| BarOpsCo, London | £160 for bartender hire up to 3 hours; £300 for a cocktail bartender with setup up to 4 hours | One supplier's price guide; alcohol supplied by client or supplier |
| The Sesh Bars, London | Dry hire from £350; all-inclusive £6.50 to £7.50 per person per hour, excluding VAT | Bar, bartenders and glassware for dry hire; travel included inside the M25 |
| Mix & Twist, 2026 guide | £20 to £90 per head for mobile bars, standard service £20 to £50 | A mobile bar company's rough guide across styles |

Poptop's pages also show platform-wide averages that differ greatly between pages, so they are not used here. Check each supplier's current page, VAT and minimums before relying on any figure.
