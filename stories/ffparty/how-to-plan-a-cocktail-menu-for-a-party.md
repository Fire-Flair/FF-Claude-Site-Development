# How to plan a cocktail menu for a party

Pick a short list that covers the main tastes, keeps the bartender's work manageable and includes good alcohol-free choices. For most parties, four to six drinks is plenty. More choice slows service for everyone.

## A balanced list

- **One long, refreshing drink** that works as the default, such as a highball or spritz style.
- **One classic with a spirit-led flavour**, for guests who like a stronger drink.
- **One sweeter or fruitier option.**
- **One bitter or dry option**, if your guests will enjoy it.
- **At least one alcohol-free cocktail**, treated as a drink and not an afterthought.

## Keep the service realistic

Drinks that are shaken, stirred or built over ice each take different amounts of time, and the more elaborate drinks slow a queue. If you expect a rush, keep most of the menu to quick builds and offer one or two showpiece drinks. This is also why [the number of bartenders](/stories/how-many-bartenders-do-i-need) depends on the menu.

## Who supplies what

Decide with your supplier whether they bring the ingredients, you do, or the cost is shared. See [what to provide when you hire a bartender](/stories/what-to-provide-when-hiring-a-bartender) and [supplying your own alcohol](/stories/supply-own-alcohol-hire-bartender).
