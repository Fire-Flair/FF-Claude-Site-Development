# FF Party vs Add to Event for bar hire

Add to Event lets you request quotes from suppliers and compare their profiles, reviews and photos. FF Party works from your event brief, and the FireFlair team helps arrange and confirm the bar. This page is published by FireFlair, which runs FF Party, and describes Add to Event from its public pages, checked on 8 November 2026.

## Side by side

| | Add to Event | FF Party |
|---|---|---|
| **How you start** | Describe your event and request quotes | Describe your event and choose your service |
| **Who you compare** | Suppliers' profiles, reviews and photos | The professionals and packages that suit your brief |
| **Who does the enquiring** | You contact and compare suppliers | The FireFlair team contacts bar companies and bartenders for larger events |
| **Supplier costs** | Suppliers buy credits to contact leads and pay no booking commission | Not applicable to you as a host |
| **What you pay** | Check each supplier's quote | A £50 deposit, fully refunded if we cannot find the staff, plus the price of the service |
| **Confirmation** | Agreed with the supplier | We confirm with you, and your booking page is the record |

## Who may suit

**Add to Event** may suit you if you enjoy comparing many quotes yourself and want to see reviews and photos from many suppliers.

**FF Party** may suit you if you want an experienced team to do the enquiring and to confirm your booking.
