# Are We Entering the Golden Age of Parties?

Think of the birthday dinner where everyone stays for one more drink, or the evening a friend learns to make their favourite cocktail. These are the kinds of moments you have to be there for. At FireFlair, we think the next wave of parties could start with more people making time for evenings like these. "Golden age" is our view, not a proven fact: the research below shows the appetite, and you can decide what it means for your next celebration.

## Why getting together matters

The World Health Organization estimates that about one in six people worldwide, or 15.8%, report feeling lonely. That gives some context for why social connection deserves attention. Source: [World Health Organization, Social connection](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/social-connection).

In July 2025, Eventbrite's research partner dcdx surveyed 4,051 people aged 18–35 in the US and UK. In the survey, 79% expected to attend more events in 2026, rising to 82% among UK respondents. A separate US study by Archrival found that 74% of Gen Z respondents put more value on in-person experiences than digital ones. Sources: [Eventbrite Social Study 2026, pp. 4 and 25](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260114088488/en); Archrival research reported by Vogue Business.

## What smaller gatherings can offer

Eventbrite reported that attendance at micro-events on its platform rose 23% in 2024. For a host, the appeal of a smaller gathering can be personal. There is room to choose a drink for the birthday guest, bring friends into the kitchen, and give everyone a chance to try their hand at making a cocktail. Source: [Eventbrite TRNDS 2025, p. 14](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Eventbrite-TRNDS-2025-UKI.pdf).

## The people who make it happen

Grand View Research estimates that the global event management market was worth US$1.16 trillion in 2024 and projects it could reach US$2.09 trillion by 2033. That estimate spans conferences, weddings, social events, sport, and live entertainment. A private celebration is one part of that much wider market. Source: [Grand View Research, Event Management Market](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/event-management-market-report).

At home, a bartender can keep drinks moving while the host catches up with friends. At a bigger celebration, DJs, caterers, planners, and venues can all play a part. Pick an occasion you'd love to celebrate and give the people you want there a date to put in the diary.
