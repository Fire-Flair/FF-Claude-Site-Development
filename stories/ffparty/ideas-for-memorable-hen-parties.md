# Ideas for memorable hen parties

A great hen party is made of shared laughter, not of schedules. The best ones leave everyone with a story, and the bride with the feeling that her friends planned something just for her.

## Choose an activity everyone can join

Pick something that works for the whole group, whatever their usual evening looks like. When everyone takes part, nobody feels left out and the photos are better.

## Add a drinks experience

A cocktail masterclass is a hen party favourite because it is social by design. Your group learns from an expert, shakes up its own drinks and tastes the results together. With the Open Bar Masterclass, everyone creates their own recipes, which makes for plenty of toasts.

## Keep the details personal

Name a cocktail after the bride, bring matching garnishes or choose a theme. Small touches make the day feel like hers.

## Leave time to simply enjoy it

Build gaps into the plan so friends can chat, laugh and take photos without watching the clock.
