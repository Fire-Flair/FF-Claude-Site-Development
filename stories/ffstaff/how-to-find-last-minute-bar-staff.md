# How to find last-minute bar staff

Send a complete brief at once, name the skills you cannot compromise on, invite several people in parallel, and treat anyone as booked only once they confirm. Speed comes from clear information, not from skipping steps.

## Write the brief in five lines

1. Date, start and finish, and exact address.
2. Role and how many people.
3. Must-have skills, for example solo bar or classic cocktails.
4. Pay for the shift.
5. Who they meet and what they wear.

A complete brief gets faster answers because professionals do not need to ask. See [how to write a brief for temporary bar staff](/stories/temporary-bar-staff-brief-template).

## Be realistic about timing

Professionals have up to 24 hours to reply to an invitation, and never beyond the shift start. That is a response window. It does not guarantee a reply or a confirmed booking within a day. For urgent shifts:

- Invite several matching professionals rather than waiting on one.
- Use Autofill alongside people you already know.
- Keep a backup plan until someone is confirmed.

Each invitation shows its real deadline, normally the earlier of seven days after creation and the shift start.

## Check it is confirmed

"Available" is not a booking. You are covered only when a professional has accepted and confirmed, or when you have chosen them. Check your booking page, which is the official record.

## If you cannot fill it

If nobody is confirmed, the unfilled Shift Card is refunded under the policy. See [fees, refunds and replacements](/stories/ff-staff-booking-fees-refunds-replacements). If someone cancels, see [what to do when an event bartender cancels](/stories/what-to-do-when-an-event-bartender-cancels).
