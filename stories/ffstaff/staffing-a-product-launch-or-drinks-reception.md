# How to staff a product launch or corporate drinks reception

Corporate receptions have a sharp arrival peak and a short window. Plan staff for the first twenty minutes, not the average, and assign clear roles.

## Worked example (fictional)

A 120-guest product launch, doors at 18:30, drinks until 21:30.

| Time | Activity | Staff |
|---|---|---|
| 16:30 | Setup: ice, glassware, garnish, stock | Bar lead plus barback |
| 18:00 | Briefing and final checks | All |
| 18:30 to 19:00 | Arrival peak | Two bartenders on the bar, barback restocking |
| 19:00 to 21:00 | Steady service | Bartenders alternate short breaks |
| 21:00 to 21:30 | Last orders and wind-down | Bar lead |
| 21:30 to 22:15 | Clear down | Barback and bartenders |

This is an illustration to adapt, not a rule.

## Decisions to make

- **Stations.** One bar or two? Two reduces queues for 120 guests.
- **Menu.** A short menu or a few batched drinks speeds service.
- **Breaks.** Schedule them so the bar is never empty at the peak.
- **Setup and close.** Count these hours in the booking.

## Roles

Decide who leads. See [barback, bartender or bar manager](/stories/barback-bartender-or-bar-manager).
