# How to write a brief for temporary bar staff, with a template

A good brief answers every question before it is asked. Copy the template below and fill it in. A clear brief gets better responses and fewer problems on the night.

## Template

**Event:** (name and type)
**Date and times:** arrival, service start, service end, finish
**Venue and access:** address, entrance, parking, loading, ID or pass needed
**Role and numbers:** (for example, two bartenders and one barback)
**Duties:** setup, service, clearing, stock handling
**Menu:** drinks to be served and anything not served
**Kit:** what is supplied and what the professional should bring
**Dress:** uniform or dress code
**Contact on the day:** name and phone
**Pay:** the agreed amount and when it is paid
**Breaks:** when and how long
**If something changes:** who to call and what happens if the event runs late

## Tips

- Put times in 24-hour form to avoid confusion.
- Name the menu. Professionals can prepare.
- State who they report to.
- Be honest about the length of the day.

## On FF Staff

Most of these items go into the fields of a Shift Card. Put anything extra in the notes. The more complete the card, the more likely it is to be filled.
