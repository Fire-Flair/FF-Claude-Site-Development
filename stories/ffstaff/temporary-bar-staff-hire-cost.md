# How much does it cost to hire temporary bar staff?

The total is the professional's pay for the hours worked, plus any booking fee or agency charge, plus tax where it applies and costs such as travel. Providers split these differently, so compare what each one charges and who pays what.

## The cost components

- **Pay** for the shift, including setup and clear-down if you agree them.
- **Hours**, because pay and many fees grow with time.
- **Booking fee or agency charge**, and how it is calculated.
- **VAT, employer costs or payroll**, depending on the arrangement.
- **Travel, uniform or equipment**, if not included.

## How providers charge

Most agencies charge by the hour: the worker is paid one rate and the client is billed a higher one. For example (invented, to show the arithmetic), if a bartender is paid £20 an hour and the agency bills you £40 an hour, a 10-hour shift costs you £400 while the bartender receives £200. The difference pays for the agency's costs, which can include payroll, insurance and administration, so it is not all profit. Percentage models work similarly: Brigad's pages describe a percentage service fee, and limber's pricing page describes a percentage fee with wages and employment costs additional.

FF Staff charges a fixed £10 booking connection fee for each Shift Card that is filled. It is not per person and not per hour, so a one-hour shift and a ten-hour shift carry the same fee, and the professional's pay is agreed with them. The same bartender at £20 an hour for 10 hours costs £200 in pay plus the £10 fee, which is £210. This is first-year launch pricing, which FireFlair has said will stay fixed for loyal users.

## Examples (fictional, to show the arithmetic)

| Booking | Pay | FF Staff fee | Total before VAT |
|---|---|---|---|
| One bartender, 6 hours at £20 an hour, one Shift Card | £120 | £10 | £130 |
| One bartender, 10 hours at £20 an hour, one Shift Card | £200 | £10 | £210 |
| Three bartenders on one Shift Card, 5 hours each at £20 an hour | £300 | £10 | £310 |
| Three bartenders and one barback on two Shift Cards (barback at £15 an hour for 5 hours) | £375 | £20 | £395 |

For comparison, the same three bartenders for 5 hours billed at £40 an hour each would be £600 before any other cost. Check each provider's current terms, because rates, VAT and minimums vary.
