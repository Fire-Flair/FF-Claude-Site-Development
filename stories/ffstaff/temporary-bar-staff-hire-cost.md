# How much does it cost to hire temporary bar staff?

The total is the professional's pay for the hours worked, plus any booking fee or agency charge, plus tax where it applies and costs such as travel. Different providers split these differently, so compare what each one charges and who pays what.

## The cost components

- **Pay** for the shift, including setup and clear-down if you agree them.
- **Hours**, because pay and many fees grow with time.
- **Booking fee or agency charge**, and how it is calculated.
- **VAT, employer costs or payroll**, depending on the arrangement.
- **Travel, uniform or equipment**, if not included.

## How providers charge

Providers use different structures. Brigad's pages describe a percentage service fee, with a separate fee for talent and for business. limber's pricing page describes a percentage fee on a plan, with wages and employment costs additional. An agency may bill an hourly client rate above the worker's pay. Because many of these grow with hours, a long shift costs more in fees as well as pay.

FF Staff charges a fixed booking charge per Shift Card, paid before invitations go out. It is not per person and not per hour, so a one-hour shift and a ten-hour shift carry the same charge. Pay for each professional is separate and agreed with them. This is first-year launch pricing, which FireFlair says will stay fixed for loyal users. VAT presentation will be shown at checkout.

## Examples (fictional, to show the arithmetic)

| Booking | Pay | FireFlair charge | Total before VAT |
|---|---|---|---|
| One bartender, 6 hours at £20 an hour, one Shift Card | £120 | £10 | £130 |
| Three bartenders on one Shift Card, 5 hours each at £20 an hour | £300 | £10 | £310 |
| Three bartenders and one barback on two Shift Cards (barback at £15 an hour for 5 hours) | £375 | £20 | £395 |

Compare an hourly client rate of £40 for the same three bartenders over 5 hours, which would be £600 before any other cost. That rate also pays the agency's own costs, so the gap is not all profit. Check each provider's current terms.
