# What Should a Bartender Be Able to Decide on a Company Booking?

A bartender can decide how service runs inside the agreed brief. Anything that changes the price, the hours or a signature recipe goes back to the company first. Agreeing that split before the booking keeps the night smooth.

## Three kinds of decision

- **Service on the night.** Timing of rounds, order of service and how the bar is set out, all within the agreed hours.
- **Choices from an approved list.** A menu option the company has already said can be offered.
- **Changes that need the company.** Price, hours, extra stock, glassware or bar hire, and anything that alters a signature recipe.

A signature recipe belongs to the company that created it. The company decides what is shared with the bartender and with the host.

## A worked scenario

This scenario is illustrative. A host books a bar company for an evening and knows the company's signature cocktail. On the day they ask about an ingredient swap, extra glassware and a later finish.

| The host asks | Who decides | Where it is recorded |
|---|---|---|
| An ingredient swap in the signature cocktail | The company, since the recipe is theirs | The booking notes, once approved |
| Extra glassware | The company, because it affects stock and cost | The booking notes and the updated quote |
| A later finish | The company, because it changes hours | The booking notes and the updated hours |
| Running the first round when guests arrive | The bartender, within the agreed hours | Nothing further needed |

## Let the host and bartender talk

Alex Gagnon, founder of FireFlair, takes the view that it works best when the bartender and the client communicate directly about the brief. The approvals above sit alongside that. The bartender answers questions about the evening, and the company keeps control of recipe, price and hours.

## Write it into the brief

Name an approver in the brief, so the bartender knows who to contact when a request falls outside the agreement. Add a line for changes: who agreed them and what the final arrangement is. Most items from the [brief template](/stories/temporary-bar-staff-brief-template) go into the fields of a Shift Card, and anything extra goes into the notes.
