# How to staff a Christmas party bar

*This guide covers what to plan and ask now. The FireFlair service described as coming soon is not yet available, and this page will be updated when it is.*


December fills early, so book your bar staff as soon as your date is fixed, and plan for a heavier first hour than usual.

## Plan

- **Date:** the most popular nights go first
- **Numbers:** guests, and how many arrive at once
- **Menu:** a short list of drinks, with something warm if you like
- **Roles:** bartenders, a barback and a lead. See [barback, bartender or bar manager](/stories/barback-bartender-or-bar-manager)
- **Late finish:** agree end time and travel home
