# Why Businesses Still Invest in In-Person Events

The Events Industry Council and Oxford Economics estimate that business events generated US$1.3 trillion in direct spending worldwide in 2025. The study covers business events across more than 180 countries. Its direct-spending measure covers activity across the business-event economy, including expenditure beyond the event venue itself. Source: [Events Industry Council, 2026 Global Economic Significance of Business Events Study](https://eventscouncil.org/Leadership/Economic-Significance-Study).

## What respondents value about meeting in person

In the same study, 70% of survey respondents identified face-to-face relationship-building as the event outcome most difficult to replace. That finding describes respondents' views. It gives venues, caterers, and agencies a business objective to listen for in a client brief, alongside separate questions about format, spend, and supplier requirements. Source: [Events Industry Council, 2026 Global Economic Significance of Business Events Study](https://eventscouncil.org/Leadership/Economic-Significance-Study).
