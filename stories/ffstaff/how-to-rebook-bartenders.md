# How to rebook bartenders and build a regular event team

The easiest way to a reliable bar team is to ask the people who did well to come back. Keep a short list of connections, give them early notice and always have a backup.

## Build the team

1. **Note who excelled.** After each event, record who was punctual, skilled and good with guests.
2. **Connect on FF Staff.** People you have worked with can be invited again by name.
3. **Keep roles consistent.** Invite the same person for the same kind of shift, so they know what to expect.
4. **Give notice.** The earlier you ask, the more likely they are free.

## Plan a backup

For any important event, invite one more person than you need, or have a second name ready. People cancel and get ill, even the best.

## Keep the record tidy

Use a new Shift Card for each date, with the same clear brief. Each filled card carries a £10 booking connection fee.
