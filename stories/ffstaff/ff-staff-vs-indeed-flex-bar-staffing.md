# FF Staff vs Indeed Flex for bar staffing

Indeed Flex is a broad temporary-work service with matching and returning-worker pools. FF Staff is built for bar and event shifts, with a flat £10 booking connection fee for each filled Shift Card. This page is published by FireFlair, which runs FF Staff, and describes Indeed Flex from its public pages, checked on 8 November 2026.

## Side by side

| | Indeed Flex | FF Staff |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Many sectors, with a bartender route | Bar and event roles |
| **How you request** | Check its current employer process | You write a Shift Card: date, time, role, requirements and pay |
| **Matching** | Employer materials describe matching and pools of returning workers | Invitations go to professionals who fit; you choose who to confirm |
| **Cost** | Check its current employer pricing | £10 booking connection fee for each filled Shift Card; the professional's pay is separate |
| **Pay and admin** | Check who pays the worker and who handles payroll | You agree pay with the professional |

## Where Indeed Flex may be the better fit

If you hire across many job types, want established processes and a large pool, Indeed Flex's breadth may suit you better.

## Where FF Staff may suit

If you need bar or event staff and want to see the people you are inviting, choose who is confirmed, and know the booking fee up front whatever the length of the shift, FF Staff may suit you.

Read [how booking fees work on FF Staff](/stories/ff-staff-booking-fees-refunds-replacements) before you compare costs.
