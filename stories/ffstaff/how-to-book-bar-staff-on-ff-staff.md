# How to book bar staff on FF Staff

You create an event, add a shift for each role, then invite people by name or let Autofill find them. FireFlair's booking connection fee of £10 applies to each Shift Card that is filled. Someone is booked only when they accept and confirm, or when you choose a professional who has said they are available.

## How the pieces fit

An Event Card holds your event. Each role you need is a Shift Card, and a Shift Card can ask for several professionals. Each position and each invitation is tracked separately. The minimum you need is a phone number, an email, the event and shift details, payment and acceptance of the terms. You do not need a company account first.

## A worked example (fictional)

*A hotel needs a drinks reception on Friday 6pm to 11pm: three bartenders and one barback.*

1. Create the Event Card with the date, place and timings.
2. Add a Shift Card for bartenders and ask for three positions. Add a second Shift Card for the barback, which has a different role and pay.
3. Add duties, hours, pay, dress code and requirements.
4. The booking connection fee is £10 for each Shift Card that is filled, so the two cards here would carry £20 in total. The pay for each professional is separate and agreed with them.
5. Choose people from the staff row, or choose Autofill.

## Choose a person, or Autofill

**By name.** The person is invited exclusively for up to 24 hours, never beyond the shift start. If they choose Accept & confirm, the position is filled with no second approval.

**Autofill.** It invites professionals who match the role and work near you, normally ten at a time. Their reply "Available" is not a booking: you see who is available and any questions, then choose. Discuss first confirms nothing and does not extend a deadline.

## How long you have to choose

Each invitation has its own deadline, normally the earlier of seven days after it was created and the shift start. A reply does not restart the seven days. Your booking page shows the real deadline.

## After confirmation

You receive the booking reference, shift details, profile link and contact details. The website is the official record, and WhatsApp and email carry notifications. See [fees, refunds and replacements](/stories/ff-staff-booking-fees-refunds-replacements) for what happens if something changes.
