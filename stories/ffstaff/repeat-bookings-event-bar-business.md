# How to get repeat bookings for your event bar business

Past customers are your easiest next booking. Keep good records, make rebooking simple and ask permission before you stay in touch.

## Keep a record

For each event, note the date, guest numbers, menu and anything the customer liked. When they come back, you can repeat what worked.

## Follow up

- Thank them within a day or two.
- Ask whether they are happy for you to contact them about future events. Respect the answer.
- Ask for a review while it is fresh.

## Make rebooking easy

Offer the same package again, or a variation, with a clear price. Mention seasonal dates such as December, Easter or summer weddings, where relevant.

## Seasonal offers

A seasonal menu or an early-booking offer gives a reason to get in touch. Keep any claim true.

## Organisations

Companies and venues often need bar staff regularly. Ask whether they would like you on their list.

This page is about customers you have already served. For winning new ones, see [how to get more mobile bar bookings](/stories/how-to-get-mobile-bar-bookings).
