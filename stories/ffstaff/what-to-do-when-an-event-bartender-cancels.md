# What to do when an event bartender cancels

Collect the facts, tell your guests' contact if the timing is tight, and ask for a replacement straight away. On FF Staff, when a confirmed professional cancels the position reopens and a replacement is sought. A replacement depends on who is available.

## Straight away

1. Note the time and the reason given.
2. Check what the professional had already prepared.
3. Decide how long you can wait.

## The replacement route

If a confirmed professional cancels, the position is reopened and a replacement is sought through the agreed process, with no further replacement-matching fee. It cannot be guaranteed, especially at short notice.

## Your own backup

Keep a second name from past events. See [how to rebook bartenders](/stories/how-to-rebook-bartenders).

## The booking fee

Read [how fees, refunds and replacements work](/stories/ff-staff-booking-fees-refunds-replacements) for what applies to your card. The website is the official record.
