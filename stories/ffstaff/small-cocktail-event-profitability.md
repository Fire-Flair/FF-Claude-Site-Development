# Can a Bar Company Make Money on a Two-Hour Cocktail Party?

Yes, when the quote covers the work around the two hours as well as the drinks. A short booking is rarely a short job, so the answer comes from counting everything that happens before service.

## Where the time goes

Alex Gagnon, founder of FireFlair, once ran a team of ten bartenders and found most of his own time went on messaging them. That is one practitioner's experience, and it points at a cost that is easy to leave out of a quote.

Before a bartender pours a drink, someone has usually:

1. Qualified the enquiry and confirmed the date, place and guest numbers.
2. Found the right person.
3. Settled the menu with the host.
4. Assigned stock and equipment.
5. Confirmed travel and arrival.
6. Sent the brief.

## A worksheet to fill in

This worksheet is illustrative. It has no figures because every business counts differently. Fill each line in from your own records.

| Line | What to include | Where the number comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Quote | What the host pays for the booking | Your agreed quote |
| Direct costs | Staff time on the night, stock, ice, glassware, consumables, travel | Your supplier and staff records |
| Contribution | Quote minus direct costs | The two lines above |
| Admin time | Quoting, matching, menu, brief, follow-up | A simple time log for the next five bookings |
| Value of admin time | Admin time at the rate you would pay for the work | Your own rate |
| Result | Contribution minus value of admin time | The lines above |

Run the worksheet on three recent small bookings. The pattern across them tells you more than any single job.

## Standardise or quote bespoke?

| Element | Often suits a standard approach | Often needs a bespoke quote |
|---|---|---|
| Menu | A set menu with defined inclusions | A new menu built around the host's recipe |
| Staffing | A fixed bartender-to-guest pattern | Unusual venue, access or hours |
| Equipment | A standard kit list | Specialist equipment or bar hire |
| Brief | One template for every booking | Details only this host has |

Standardising a part of the job means fewer messages and quicker quotes. Keep bespoke quotes for the parts where the host wants something different.
