# Barback, bartender or bar manager: who should you hire?

A bartender makes and serves drinks, a barback keeps the bar stocked and clean, and a bar manager runs the bar and its team. The right mix depends on the size and style of the event.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Typical responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Barback** | Ice, glassware, restocking, clearing, keeping the bar clean |
| **Bartender** | Taking orders, making and serving drinks, handling payment where relevant |
| **Bar manager** | Planning the bar, stock, team, timings and problem-solving |

Agencies such as Off to Work and Wilde Toast Events advertise these roles, among others.

## Scenarios

- **Small private party:** one experienced bartender may be enough.
- **Mid-sized event:** a bartender plus support.
- **Large reception:** several bartenders, one or more barbacks and a lead.

## A lead is not always a manager

Naming someone as the contact on the day is different from paying someone to manage. If you want management duties, say so in the brief and agree the pay for them.
