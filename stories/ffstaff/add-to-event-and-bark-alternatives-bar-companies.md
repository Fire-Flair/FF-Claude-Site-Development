# Add to Event and Bark alternatives for bar companies

Lead marketplaces for event suppliers usually charge in one of three ways: you pay for leads, you pay commission on bookings, or the customer pays a booking fee. Which suits your bar company depends on how many leads turn into jobs.

## Three models

| Model | You pay | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Pay for leads** | For each enquiry or lead credit | You pay whether or not you win the job |
| **Commission** | A share of confirmed bookings | Cost rises with larger bookings |
| **Customer-paid booking fee** | Nothing to list; the customer pays a fee | Fewer leads may reach you |

Check each service's current terms, since they change. This page does not rate any named service.

## Measure lead quality

For any source, track:

1. Enquiries received
2. Enquiries that fit your service and area
3. Quotes sent
4. Bookings confirmed
5. Cost per confirmed booking

A cheap lead that never books costs more than a dearer one that does.
