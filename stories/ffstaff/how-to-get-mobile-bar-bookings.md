# How to get more bookings for your mobile bar business

Most mobile bar businesses get work from a mix of their own website, referrals, marketplaces and listings. Each has a different cost in time and money, and each brings a different kind of customer. Measure which actually produces bookings.

## Channels

| Channel | Strength | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| **Your website** | You own it and control the message | Takes time to be found |
| **Referrals** | Warm, trusted leads | Hard to scale |
| **Marketplaces** | Customers already searching | Compare how each charges: for leads, commission or booking fees |
| **FireFlair listing** | Customers planning an event on FF Party | Check what is currently available for suppliers |

Keep your claims to what you can show.

## A profile checklist

- Clear photos of your bar and your work, with permission
- What is included: staff, hours, drinks and equipment
- Areas you cover
- A price starting point, or what affects the price
- Reviews from real customers
- A quick way to ask a question

## Measure

Count enquiries, quotes and confirmed bookings from each source, so you know where your time pays off.
