# How to sell a signature cocktail service through FF Party

*This guide covers what to plan and ask now. The FireFlair service described as coming soon is not yet available, and this page will be updated when it is.*


A signature cocktail service works best when it is specific: a named menu, clear quantities and a recipe you can repeat.

## Prepare now

- Choose a small set of signature drinks
- Write recipes you can scale
- Work out what you need for a given guest number
- Decide what is included and what is not. See [writing a package](/stories/how-to-write-a-mobile-bar-package)
