# Bar staffing agency or freelance bartender platform

A managed agency usually supplies and manages the staff for you. A freelance platform lets you choose from individual professionals. FireFlair connects you with professionals and handles the booking, and the pay and working arrangement are agreed between you and each professional. Your choice depends on how much choosing, managing and responsibility you want.

## What differs

| | Managed agency | Freelance platform |
|---|---|---|
| **Who selects** | The agency, to your brief | You, from profiles, or the platform matches |
| **Support** | Often a named account contact and cover | Varies by platform |
| **Payroll and pay** | Often handled by the agency | Varies; some platforms handle payment, others leave it to you |
| **Cost shape** | May be an hourly client rate | May be a percentage fee, or a fixed fee per booking |
| **Responsibility** | Depends on the arrangement | Depends on the arrangement |

Employment status and liability depend on the actual working arrangement, not on what a contract calls it. Check current Acas and government guidance, or take advice, before you rely on any one model.

## When an agency may fit

- You need a large team or multiple events at once.
- You want a single contact who takes responsibility for supply.
- You want payroll and compliance handled for you.

Examples include general hospitality agencies and bar specialists such as Off to Work, Wilde Toast, Viking Staffing & Events and Flair.

## When a platform may fit

- You want to see and choose individuals.
- You want to rebook the same people.
- You want costs that are clear up front.
