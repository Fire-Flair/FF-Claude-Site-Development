# Indeed Flex alternatives for hiring bar staff

If you hire bar staff and are weighing up Indeed Flex, the right alternative depends on what you need: repeat teams, wide coverage, large volume or simple one-off bookings. This page is published by FireFlair, which runs FF Staff, and describes other services from their public pages, checked on 8 November 2026.

## What Indeed Flex offers

Indeed Flex covers many sectors and has a dedicated bartender route. Its employer materials describe matching and pools of returning workers. Its breadth and established processes are real strengths for businesses that hire across several job types.

## Routes to compare

| Route | What it is | Worth checking |
|---|---|---|
| **Brigad** | Hospitality missions with contracts, invoicing and support | Its business page describes a 10% mission fee |
| **limber** | Hospitality staffing with favourite worker teams, payment and payroll handling | Its Classic pricing page states a 10% fee, with wages and employment costs additional |
| **Stint** | Recurring teams on short shifts | Current help material describes a commitment of at least three months |
| **Bar specialists** | Off to Work, Wilde Toast Events, Viking Staffing & Events, AGN Events | Bar-focused teams, with a range of specialist roles |
| **Large-event staffing** | Flair, hap Solutions | Suited to big deployments. Check location and vacancies |
| **FF Staff** | Shift Cards for bar and event roles | A £10 booking connection fee for each filled Shift Card |

## Choosing

- **Regular shifts all year:** a recurring-team service may fit.
- **Large events:** a company with large-deployment experience may fit.
- **One-off or occasional bar shifts:** a card-based request may fit.
- **You want payroll handled for you:** compare who becomes the employer on each service.

Ask each one the same questions: who pays the worker, what you pay in total, how replacements work and how you are told about changes.
