# FF Staff vs Brigad for hospitality staffing

Brigad is a hospitality freelance platform with contracts, invoicing, payment options, insurance and support. FF Staff is a card-based booking service for bar and event shifts. This page is published by FireFlair, which runs FF Staff, and describes Brigad from its public pages, checked on 8 November 2026.

## What the business pays

| | Brigad | FF Staff |
|---|---|---|
| **Business fee** | Its business page describes a 10% mission fee | £10 booking connection fee for each filled Shift Card |
| **What the fee is based on** | A percentage, so it moves with the size of the mission | A flat amount, so a short shift and a long shift carry the same fee |
| **Pay to the worker** | Check how it is set and paid | Agreed with the professional |

Compare the service supplied alongside the fee, not only the number. Brigad lists contracts, invoicing and insurance as part of its offer, so check what each service handles for you.

## Matching and records

Check how each service finds people and what record you keep afterwards. On FF Staff the website holds the official record of each card, with notifications by WhatsApp and email.

## Worker fees (kept separate)

Brigad's talent help page states a 12.9% service fee including VAT for workers. That is paid by the worker, not by you, though it can affect what workers expect.

## Which may suit

A managed service with insurance may suit a business that wants that bundle. A flat booking fee and direct agreement with the professional may suit a business that wants simple one-off bookings.
