# How to write a mobile bar package customers can compare

Customers compare packages side by side, so state exactly what is included and what is not. A clear package gets more enquiries and fewer arguments later.

## What to cover

- **Staff:** how many, and their experience
- **Hours:** service time, and setup and clear-down
- **Drinks:** what is on the menu, and whether stock is included
- **Equipment:** bar, glassware, ice, tools
- **Exclusions:** what the customer must provide
- **Travel:** the area covered and any extra for distance
- **Price conditions:** what changes the price, such as guest numbers or hours

## Example (fictional)

> **Classic Cocktail Bar, up to 60 guests.** One experienced bartender and one assistant for four hours of service. Includes a mobile bar, glassware, ice and a menu of four cocktails. Stock supplied on request. Excludes venue licence. Travel included within 20 miles.

Adapt it to your own service and do not copy it.
