# What VisitBritain's Data Shows About Small Business Meetings

VisitBritain estimates that meetings of 10–20 people accounted for 772,000 inbound UK visits and £794 million in associated visitor spending in 2024. That is about 30% of the 2.56 million inbound business-event visits in its table. Source: [VisitBritain, Business Events Research](https://www.visitbritain.org/research-insights/business-events-research).

## What counts as a visit

VisitBritain's table measures trips to the UK and spending associated with those trips. A meeting with several international attendees can account for several visits. The spending figure covers visitor spending across the trip. For venues and caterers, the useful distinction is the scale of travel associated with this meeting size. Domestic meetings, venue bookings, and bar sales each need their own data. Source: [VisitBritain, Business Events Research](https://www.visitbritain.org/research-insights/business-events-research).

## Using it in your planning

These are inbound visits, so they say nothing about UK residents meeting in the UK. If you host or cater for small meetings and receptions, the figures are context for the size of the audience, not a forecast for your own bookings.
