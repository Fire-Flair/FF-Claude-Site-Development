# What the Events Growth Forecast Measures

Grand View Research estimates the global event management market at US$1.16 trillion in 2024 and forecasts US$2.09 trillion by 2033. Its scope includes meetings and conferences, weddings, social events, sport, music, and entertainment. The forecast describes the combined market across those categories. Source: [Grand View Research, Event Management Market](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/event-management-market-report).

## A separate measure of smaller events

Eventbrite's TRNDS 2025 report records a 23% rise in micro-event attendance on its platform in 2024. That figure counts attendance within a platform category. Grand View Research's figures describe market value in US dollars. Read together, they give event businesses two distinct reference points for scale and audience activity. Source: [Eventbrite TRNDS 2025, p. 14](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Eventbrite-TRNDS-2025-UKI.pdf).

## What not to read into them

A forecast is a modelled estimate, and a platform's attendance is recorded activity on that platform. Neither is a measure of demand for bar staff at a given venue or in a given postcode. Use them as context for planning, and use your own booking history for staffing numbers.
