# WhatsApp reply wording

Every Shift Card, on the website and in the WhatsApp template, offers the same three buttons, for both booking types:

| Button | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Available | On a shift chosen for you (exclusive), confirms it at once and offers Add to calendar. On an open shift (Autofill) it puts you on the list; the booker chooses. |
| Unavailable | Declines the shift. |
| More info | Opens the shift brief at `ffteam.uk/fx/<code>`. Asking a question is offered on the brief page. |

The card image is 5 x 7 (1080 x 1512). WhatsApp prints the time over the bottom right corner, so the footer keeps the logo and address on the left.
Templates need provider approval; Core owns the reply handling.
